From a real walk to a virtual journey

WalkQuest reads your step count only after permission and converts movement into progress on the route you selected.

  • Connect Health Connect — Enable read-only step access in Settings. Other health information is not needed.
  • Walk as usual — Your synchronized steps become route distance. About 1,250 steps equal one virtual kilometre before bonuses.
  • Watch the map advance — The walker marker moves between route stops, and the next destination shows how many kilometres remain.
