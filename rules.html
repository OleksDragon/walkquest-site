How to travel with your real steps

Learn how the map moves, where to see your position, how cities open, and what happens when your route reaches water.

👟 1,250 ≈ 1 km📍 Cities unlock automatically🔒 No account required
Start moving

From a real walk to a virtual journey

WalkQuest reads your step count only after permission and converts movement into progress on the route you selected.

01♥

Connect Health Connect

Enable read-only step access in Settings. Other health information is not needed.

02👟

Walk as usual

Your synchronized steps become route distance. About 1,250 steps equal one virtual kilometre before bonuses.

03🗺️

Watch the map advance

The walker marker moves between route stops, and the next destination shows how many kilometres remain.

Reading the map

Your position is always visible

The walking marker is your current calculated position. City pins show destinations along the route.

  • Current position. Tap the walker to open Street View near the closest route point.
  • City markers. Reached cities become available; future stops remain locked until you arrive.
  • Map exploration. Opened places can launch Google Maps or nearby Street View in an external app.
City discovery

What opens when you reach a city

Every unlocked stop becomes a small travel guide, not just a checkpoint.

📖

Read the city story

See a localized introduction, an interesting fact, and the city’s place in your route.

📍

Explore must-see places

Open landmarks, view their descriptions, and jump to Maps or Street View when available.

🍜

Discover local food

City cards include regional dishes and short explanations of what makes them special.

📔

Add a passport stamp

Save the city to your travel passport and share the collected stamp as a card.

Water and long crossings

When the road becomes a ferry, train, or flight

At a special transfer gate, walking progress pauses at the shore or terminal. Choose how to start the crossing.

✨

Premium

The crossing is included, costs no travel points, and uses a one-day travel time.

🧭

Travel points

Spend the number of points shown on the gate and wait for the route’s travel time.

▶️

Rewarded videos

Free players can complete the required rewarded views instead of spending points.

🎫

Google Play ticket

Where available, buy the matching train, ferry, or flight ticket for a one-day crossing.

Steps walked while a transfer is waiting are banked, so your effort is not lost and can continue the journey after arrival.
