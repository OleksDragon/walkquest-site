Share every WalkQuest milestone — now free

Achievements, passport progress, routes, and distance milestones can now be shared without Premium.

October 3, 2026•3 min read

Your progress deserves to travel farther than the app. Sharing is now available to every WalkQuest player, so a completed route, a new stamp, or a hard-earned achievement can invite friends to start walking too.

Sharing belongs to everyone

The share action is no longer a Premium feature. Use the apps installed on your phone to publish an achievement, route progress, passport milestone, or distance record.

One post, two useful destinations

Shared messages include links to the WalkQuest website and Google Play. UTM and Play referrer tags show how many visits and installs come from community posts.

Measurement without changing your story

Campaign tags describe the sharing channel and campaign. They do not add your name, steps, location, or achievement details to the link.

Every shared milestone can be the first step of somebody else’s journey.
